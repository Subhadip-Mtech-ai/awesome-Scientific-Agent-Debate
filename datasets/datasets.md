# Datasets and Benchmarks

Datasets and evaluation benchmarks referenced or relevant to the paper's discussion of single-LLM and multi-agent debate performance on reasoning and hypothesis-generation tasks.

## Reasoning and Factuality Benchmarks (used in MAD evaluation literature)

| Dataset | Domain | Used By / Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| **GSM8K** (Grade School Math 8K) | Arithmetic word-problem reasoning | Standard MAD benchmark (Du et al., 2023; Hegazy, 2024; Liang et al., 2024) |
| **MMLU** (Massive Multitask Language Understanding) | Broad knowledge/reasoning across 57 subjects | Used in debate-failure-mode studies (Wynn, Satija & Hadfield, 2025) |
| **CommonsenseQA** | Commonsense reasoning | Used in Wynn et al. (2025); shown to be harmed by debate in some configurations |
| **ASDiv** (Academia Sinica Diverse MWP Dataset) | Diverse arithmetic word problems | Heterogeneous-agent debate SOTA claim (Hegazy, 2024) |
| **HellaSwag** | Commonsense sentence completion | Used in majority-voting-vs-debate comparison (Choi, Zhu & Li, 2025) |
| **HH-RLHF** | Helpfulness/harmlessness preference data | Used in majority-voting-vs-debate comparison (Choi, Zhu & Li, 2025) |
| **Professional medicine subset (MMLU)** | Medical knowledge QA | Used in majority-voting-vs-debate comparison (Choi, Zhu & Li, 2025) |

## Scientific Hypothesis-Generation and Discovery Evaluation

| Resource | Description | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **HeurekaBench** | Benchmark of open-ended research questions grounded in real published studies and code repositories, for evaluating agentic scientific-analysis systems end-to-end | Panigrahi, Videnović & Brbić (2026), arXiv:2601.01678 |
| **100+ NLP Researcher Idea-Generation Study** | Blind human-expert review dataset comparing LLM-generated vs. expert-written research ideas across novelty and feasibility | Si, Yang & Hashimoto (2024), arXiv:2409.04109 |
| **SciAgents Materials Knowledge Graph** | Ontological knowledge graph built from ~1,000 materials-science papers used for graph-based hypothesis traversal | Ghafarollahi & Buehler (2025), DOI:10.1002/adma.202413523 |
| **AI co-scientist Validation Cases** | Three biomedical validation domains: drug repurposing, novel target discovery, bacterial gene transfer/antimicrobial resistance mechanism | Gottweis et al. (2025), arXiv:2502.18864 |
| **Robin dAMD Case Study** | In-vitro validated hypothesis (ripasudil for dry age-related macular degeneration) generated by the Robin multi-agent system | Ghareeb et al. (2025), DOI:10.1038/s41586-026-10652-y |
| **Coated-LLM Alzheimer's Combination Dataset** | In-vitro validated combinatorial drug hypothesis (m266 antibody + Gypenoside XVII) for amyloid-beta aggregation inhibition | *iScience* (2025), cell.com/iscience/S2589-0042(25)02245-X |

## Notes on Access

- Benchmark datasets (GSM8K, MMLU, CommonsenseQA, HellaSwag, ASDiv, HH-RLHF) are publicly available via Hugging Face Datasets or their original release repositories.
- Case-study "datasets" from applied hypothesis-generation systems (AI co-scientist, SciAgents, Robin, Coated-LLM) are not standardized benchmarks — they are individual validated case studies reported in the cited publications and should be treated as illustrative evidence rather than reusable evaluation sets.
- No dataset files are stored in this repository; this file is a pointer index only. Consult each source publication for licensing and access terms before reuse.
